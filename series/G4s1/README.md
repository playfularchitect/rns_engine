# G4 Series 1 — Frozen Public Archive

Series 1 is the first frozen public `rns_engine` research and certification release produced by private G4.

This directory serves as the canonical index for Series 1.

The original public runtime, replay, evidence, and manifest files remain in their existing repository locations so previously released installs, links, and reproducibility paths continue to work unchanged.

## Archive map

- [`evidence/`](evidence/) — frozen Series 1 evidence and claim boundaries.
- [`benchmark/`](benchmark/) — Series 1 benchmark documentation and entry points.
- [`replay/`](replay/) — public Series 1 replay and runtime information.
- [`manifests/`](manifests/) — frozen shape, runtime, source, and certification manifests.
- [`FROZEN_RELEASE.md`](FROZEN_RELEASE.md) — Series 1 release identity, preservation status, and repository references.

## Series 1 status

Series 1 is frozen.

Its published evidence, results, and claim boundaries are preserved as the historical record of the first G4 Series.

Future Series may improve upon Series 1, but they will not alter its frozen record.

## G4 privacy

G4 itself is private.

Series 1 contains the `rns_engine` artifacts and evidence available for public use, inspection, and replay.
